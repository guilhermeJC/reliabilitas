**Brayton-cycle** machines: they compress atmospheric air, burn fuel in the combustor and **expand the hot gases** in the power section — for power generation and mechanical drive of large compressors (class **GT** of ISO 14224:2016; API 616).

## Usual types

*(handbooks per type in Phase 1)*

- **Industrial (heavy-duty)** — designed for continuous stationary operation: rugged, long intervals, lower simple-cycle efficiency (recovered in combined cycle);
- **Aeroderivative** — an aero-engine core adapted for ground duty: light, fast start, module-swap maintenance — typical of offshore platforms and grid peaking.

Boundary note: the set's own axial compressor is an integral part of the machine (a subunit), not a process [[compressores|compressor]].

Reliability: life is dictated by the **hot section** (blades and combustors — creep, thermomechanical fatigue, hot corrosion); inlet air filtration and fuel quality are the dominant process variables.
