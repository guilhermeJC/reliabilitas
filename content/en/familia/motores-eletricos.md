Machines that **convert electrical energy into shaft work** — by far the dominant driver in the process industries (class **EM** of ISO 14224:2016; design and testing per IEC 60034 / NEMA MG-1).

## Usual types

- **Induction, squirrel-cage rotor** — the industrial workhorse: rugged, brushless, low cost. The **Three-Phase Induction Motor** handbook is one of the MVP's 5 assets *(in production)*;
- **Induction, wound rotor (slip rings)** — starting with external resistance: high starting torque on heavy loads;
- **Synchronous** — large ratings and power-factor correction; separate excitation;
- **Direct current** — legacy fine speed control, today largely replaced by induction + variable frequency drives.

Reliability: bearings (including VFD-induced shaft currents), stator insulation (thermal — every 10 °C above class halves insulation life, Montsinger's rule) and rotor bars dominate the failure history.
