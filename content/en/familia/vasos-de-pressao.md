Vessels that **operate above atmospheric pressure** — the second largest static group in the process industries. Vertical or horizontal cylindrical, spherical or special geometries; welded construction in carbon steel, low-alloy or stainless steel depending on service.

## Usual types

- **Process vessels** — gas-liquid separators, reactors, distillation columns.
- **Accumulators and surge drums** — dampen pulsations and flow misalignment.
- **Pressurized storage vessels** — LPG, compressed air, industrial gases.

Reliability: **internal corrosion** (H₂S, CO₂, chlorides), **stress cracking** (SCC, HIC), creep in high-temperature service and fatigue from thermal or pressure cycling. Risk-based inspection (API 580/581) is the dominant practice.
