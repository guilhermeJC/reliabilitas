Devices **with no moving parts** installed inside the pipe, driving mixing by successive splitting, rotation and recombination of the flow. Mixing energy comes entirely from the **head loss** the internal elements impose on the fluid — no motor, seal or bearing.

## Characteristics

- **Applications**: continuous in-line mixing, additive dilution, homogenization of products with small viscosity difference.
- **Common geometries**: alternating helical elements (Kenics), crossed-bar elements (SMX), flat-blade elements (SMV) for gases.
- **Central trade-off**: mixing quality × head loss. More elements = better mixing + higher pumping energy.

Reliability: **fouling and deposits** between elements, corrosion-erosion in particulate service, integrity of the joint with the body (welded or bolted). High service life — maintenance is dominated by periodic internal inspection rather than replacement.
