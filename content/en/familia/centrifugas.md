**Rotating** machines that artificially accelerate the gravity field through high-speed rotation — typically 3,000 to 10,000 g. They differ from [[ciclones|Cyclones]] precisely because they have moving parts: where the cyclone uses only the flow, the centrifuge uses a rotor.

## Usual types

- **Decanter** (*horizontal decanter centrifuge*) — horizontal cylinder with an internal screw that dewaters solids continuously; standard for sewage sludge, crude oil, mining.
- **Disc-stack** — a stack of conical discs that amplify the gravity-separation area; liquid-liquid separation in oils, dairy, biodiesel.
- **Basket** — batch; crystals, coarse solids.

Reliability: **main bearings at high speed**, imbalance from uneven wear of internal parts, erosion from processed solids, structural integrity of the bowl. Vibration as the primary diagnostic Fw (long P-F). API 613 governs centrifuges in severe process service.
