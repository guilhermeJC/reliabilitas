Large-diameter vessels operating at **atmospheric pressure** (or near) — the backbone of liquid storage in refineries, terminals and chemical plants. Designed to **API 650** and maintained per **API 653** (risk-based inspection).

## Usual types

- **Fixed roof** — cone or dome; low-volatility products and heavy oils.
- **External floating roof** (EFR) — floats in contact with the product; reduces vapor emissions.
- **Internal floating roof** (IFR) — floats under a fixed roof; combines protection and emissions control.

Reliability: **bottom corrosion** (plate in contact with soil/accumulated water), **CUI** (corrosion under insulation) on the shell, fatigue of nozzles from foundation differential settlement. Typical internal inspection every 10–20 years (API 653, adjusted by RBI).
