Machines for **high gas flow with moderate pressure rise** — the range between the fan and the compressor (pressure ratio ~1.1–1.2). In this window the gas is no longer strictly incompressible, but compression does not yet demand interstage cooling.

## Usual types

*(handbooks per type in Phase 1)*

- **Roots (lobes)** — positive displacement with no internal compression: near-constant flow, relief valve required;
- **Multistage centrifugal** — dynamic, continuous aeration and process-blowing service;
- **Side channel / regenerative** — peripheral: small flows at pressures above a fan's, with no metal-to-metal contact.

Signature application: **pneumatic conveying of solids** — grain, cement, polymers, sugar, flour — where air is the vehicle (dilute or dense phase). In such services, failure recording under ISO 14224 follows class **CO** (compressors) practice, since the standard defines no dedicated blower class.

Reliability: lobe timing and clearances (Roots), particulate abrasion in pneumatic conveying, and bearings dominate.
