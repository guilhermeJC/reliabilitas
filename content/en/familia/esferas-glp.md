Spherical vessels dedicated to storing **liquefied gases under pressure** — LPG, propane, butane, ammonia. The spherical geometry is the most structurally efficient solution for internal pressure: for a given pressure, the required wall thickness is half that of a cylinder of the same diameter.

## Characteristics

- **Typical volumes**: 1,000 to 12,000 m³.
- **Design pressure**: 15 to 18 barg (LPG conditions).
- **Construction**: prefabricated hemispheres field-welded onto columns with thermal expansion systems.
- **Safety**: mandatory water deluge cooling system, duplicated relief valves, lightning grounding.

Reliability: **external corrosion of support columns** (often CUI), fatigue at pipe connections, degradation of the deluge system (frequently a hidden function — periodic proof testing is mandatory). BLEVE is the catastrophic scenario that drives the entire maintenance strategy.
