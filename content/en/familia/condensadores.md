Exchangers specifically designed to **condense vapor** — transfer occurs by **latent heat**, much higher per unit mass than sensible. Relatively compact areas for large rejected heat. Ubiquitous at distillation column tops, Rankine power cycles and industrial refrigeration.

## Configurations

- **Horizontal shell-and-tube** — vapor in the shell (condenses on tubes), coolant in the tubes; process standard.
- **Vertical** — vapor down the tubes; better drainage, application with non-condensables.
- **Air-cooled condenser (ACC)** — direct condensation with air; thermal power plants in arid regions.
- **Barometric** — direct contact with water under deep vacuum.

Reliability: **non-condensables accumulation** (destroys the overall coefficient — needs adequate venting), corrosion by dissolved gases in the condensate, inadequate sub-cooling causing pump-out in cycles, drain connection integrity. Vacuum efficiency (in column-top condensers) is an early sign of trouble.
