The element that **manages the flow** — blocking, throttling, directing, protecting and controlling — without adding energy to the fluid (class **VA** of ISO 14224:2016). Every valve in throttling service pays for it in **head loss**.

## Types by function

*(handbooks per type in Phase 1)*

- **Block (on-off)** — gate, ball, plug, knife gate: full bore, tight shutoff; not made for throttling;
- **Regulation** — globe (the classic control geometry), needle (precision at small flows), diaphragm and pinch (corrosive/abrasive fluids — the body never touches the process), butterfly (large diameters, block AND coarse regulation);
- **Unidirectional protection** — check valves (swing, piston, dual plate): prevent reverse flow; their failure is **hidden** by nature;
- **Relief and safety** — PSV/PRV (API 526): the last barrier against overpressure — the classic hidden function, proven by bench proof testing;
- **Self-actuated (regulating)** — pressure reducing and flow regulating: they operate without an external actuator, using the process's own energy. Under ISO 14224 they belong to class VA — **"regulators" are not a separate family**;
- **Control** — the final element of the loop (IEC 60534), with actuator and positioner. The **Control Valve** handbook is one of the MVP's 5 assets *(in production)*.

Reliability: seat sealing (internal passing), stem sticking, actuator/positioner and the mismatch between valve and process (cavitation and flashing in severe service) dominate the history.
