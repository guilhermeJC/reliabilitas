Equipment that drives **drastic reduction of size** of particles or droplets in an emulsion or suspension, producing stability at the micrometer or nanometer scale. They differ from [[agitadores|Agitators]] by orders of magnitude in energy dissipated per unit volume — they are not for mixing, they are for **microstructure transformation**.

## Usual types

- **High pressure** (100–1500 bar) — the product is forced through a narrow gap (valve or orifice), undergoing controlled cavitation and intense shear.
- **Rotor-stator** — head with a high-speed rotor near a perforated stator; localized shear forces.
- **Ultrasonic** — cavitation induced by high-intensity ultrasonic waves; lab scale or small production.

Reliability: **homogenization valve wear** (the dominant mode — replaced by operating hours), sealing in high-pressure service, contamination by metallic particles from wear itself. Standard in the food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic industries.
