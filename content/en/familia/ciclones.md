Separators that leverage the **centrifugal force** generated by spiraling flow inside a conical-cylindrical body. **No moving parts**: the fluid motion itself does the work. Rugged, cheap and maintenance-simple — the workhorse of industrial particulate separation.

## Usual types

- **Gas cyclones** — separate dust from gas streams: particulate capture in boilers, cement, dryers.
- **Hydrocyclones** — separate solids from liquids or liquid-liquid streams of different densities; common in mining, water treatment and petroleum production.

Reliability: **erosion of the conical wall and apex** by the processed material itself (the dominant mode — service life in operating hours, not years), lower-outlet plugging, integrity of the tangential-inlet flanges. Maintenance driven by ultrasonic thickness monitoring.
