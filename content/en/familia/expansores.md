Machines that **recover work from the expansion of pressurized process gases** — and, frequently, the main product is not the work but the **cold**: the temperature drop of the near-isentropic expansion is the heart of cryogenic processes (air separation, natural gas processing, LNG).

## Usual types

*(handbooks per type in Phase 1)*

- **Turboexpander** — high-speed radial, usually in an *expander-compressor* arrangement (API 617 Part 4): class **TE** of ISO 14224:2016;
- **Piston expander** — reciprocating volumetric, small cryogenic capacities;
- **Screw expander** — rotary volumetric: waste-heat recovery and ORC cycles, tolerates wet vapor.

Reliability: bearings (magnetic or gas in turboexpanders), liquid/hydrate formation across the expansion and axial thrust balance dominate; start-up and trip are the critical transients.
