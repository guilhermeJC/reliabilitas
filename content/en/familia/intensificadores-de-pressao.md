A family distinct from [[bombas|Pumps]] within the class [[transferencia-de-fluidos-liquidos|Fluid Transfer — Liquids]]. While a pump **moves** liquid between process points, the intensifier **multiplies the pressure** of a fluid — its primary function is to raise pressure, not to transport volume.

Principle: a **differential piston** with unequal areas receives a low-pressure fluid on the large face and delivers high pressure on the small face; the intensification ratio equals the area ratio ($P_2/P_1 = A_1/A_2$). Applications: hydraulic presses, waterjet cutting, high-pressure hydrostatic testing.

They belong to this family because they add energy (pressure) to a liquid, but they are **not** pumps in the Hydraulic Institute sense — hence they sit separately, not as a principle under Pumps. *(types in Phase 1)*
