Machines that **expand boiler-generated steam** and convert the enthalpy drop into shaft work — driving generators, pumps and compressors (class **ST** of ISO 14224:2016; API 611 for general purpose, API 612 for special purpose).

## Usual arrangements

*(handbooks per type in Phase 1)*

- **Condensing** — expands down to condenser vacuum: maximum energy extraction per kg of steam;
- **Back-pressure** — discharges into a process header: cogeneration (work + useful steam);
- **Extraction** — controlled bleeds at intermediate pressures for the process;
- **Reheat** — steam returns to the boiler between stages: high-power cycles.

Reliability: moisture erosion in the last stages, deposits from silica/salt carry-over, blade fatigue and the **overspeed trip** system — the classic hidden function, proven only by periodic proof testing.
