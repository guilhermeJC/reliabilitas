The **workhorse** of industrial heat exchange. A tube bundle housed inside a cylindrical shell: one fluid flows through the tubes, the other around them in the shell. Simple in concept, unlimited in variants — nearly every refinery and chemical plant service fits into it.

## TEMA classification

Three-letter nomenclature: **front | shell | rear** (e.g. BEM = front type B, shell type E, rear type M). Dominant decisions are:

- **Shell**: E (one pass), F (two-pass), J (split flow), K (kettle — reboiler with liquid pool).
- **Rear**: fixed (S, M, U-tube) or removable (T, W — free thermal expansion).

Reliability: **tube corrosion** (the dominant mode — cooling water, chlorides, H₂S), **flow-induced vibration fatigue** (FIV, common in shells with many tubes), fouling reducing calculated U, tubesheet integrity. Maintenance dominated by periodic **hydrojetting** and tube-by-tube leak testing with eddy current.
