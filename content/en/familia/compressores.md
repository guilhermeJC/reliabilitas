Machines that **add energy to gases and vapors** — raising pressure and driving flow. The practical boundary with blowers and fans is the pressure ratio: above ~1.2 the service is a compressor's and gas compressibility dominates the design (discharge temperature becomes a process and integrity variable).

## Working principles

*(principles as their own notes and handbooks in Phase 1)*

- **Positive displacement** — discrete gas volumes are trapped and compressed:
  - *Reciprocating*: piston (API 618), diaphragm — high ratios per stage, pulsating flow;
  - *Rotary*: screw (dry/flooded), vanes, scroll, Roots (lobes), liquid ring.
- **Dynamic** — energy transferred continuously by a rotor (API 617): centrifugal (the most common in process service), axial (high flows) and mixed flow.

The working fluid is an attribute of the service, not a taxonomy level: **vapor compressors** (centrifugal, screw, piston) are the backbone of industrial refrigeration and follow the same principles.

Reliability: in reciprocating machines, compressor valves and rods/packing dominate; in dynamic machines, gas sealing (*dry gas seals*), bearings and **surge** — the unstable boundary of the curve.
