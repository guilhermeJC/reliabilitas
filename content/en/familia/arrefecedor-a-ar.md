Exchanger in which **ambient air is the cold fluid**, driven by axial fans over a **finned** tube bundle (to compensate for air's low convective coefficient). Removes cooling-water demand — a decisive argument in arid regions or under environmental restriction. Designed to **API 661**.

## Configurations

- **Forced draft** — fan below, pushes air through the bundle; standard.
- **Induced draft** — fan above, pulls air; better distribution, less recirculation.
- **Wet-fin / humidifying** — upstream misting to reduce dry-bulb temperature during heat peaks.

Reliability: **fan imbalance and bearing wear** (dominant), **fin fouling/deposits** (drastically reduces U), tube corrosion, header joint integrity. One of the few pieces of equipment whose most critical failure mode is not in the process fluid but in the **auxiliary air machine**.
