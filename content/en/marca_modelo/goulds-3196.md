## Identification

Horizontal single-stage overhung process pump (OH1, foot-mounted), *back pull-out*, by **ITT Goulds Pumps** — in continuous production since 1961 and now in the **i-FRAME** generation (bearing housing with embedded monitoring). It is the archetype of **ASME B73.1**: standardized dimensions interchangeable across manufacturers and generations — the spare-parts argument that sustains the "ANSI pump" standard in the Americas.

## Position on the six axes

| Axis | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Hydraulics | Radial (single-stage, low $n_q$) |
| Stages | 1 |

| Sealing | Sealed — seal chamber for B73.1/API 682 plans |
| Standard | ASME B73.1 |
| Service | Chemical and general industrial process |

## Ranges and construction

| Parameter | Catalog value |
| --- | --- |
| Flow | up to ~1,590 m³/h (7,000 gpm) |
| Head | up to ~223 m (730 ft) |
| Temperature | up to 371 °C (700 °F) |
| Pressure | up to ~26 bar (375 psig) |

Back pull-out construction (the rotating assembly comes out without disturbing the piping), open impeller with external clearance adjustment (light-solids and corrosive services), a wide materials chart (ductile, 316, CD4MCu, nickel alloys). The i-FRAME generation adds a vibration/temperature sensor on the bearing housing and an extended power-end warranty.

## Where it shines / limitations

**Shines** as the chemical workhorse: a giant installed population, parts anywhere, failure history documented over decades — the lowest-risk choice for B73.1 service. **Limitations**: it is a B73.1 platform — for hot/high-pressure hydrocarbons the step up is an API 610 OH2 ([[flowserve-hpx|e.g., Flowserve HPX]]); the open impeller gives up some efficiency versus closed designs.

## Reliability

Pump manufacturers do not publish catalog MTBF — the hard data comes from the field. For the US B73.1 population the reference is **~2.5 years average MTBF, 4.5+ for excellence** (Budris — see "Reliability in numbers" in the [[bomba-centrifuga|Centrifugal Pump]] handbook). The 3196 is frequently the *baseline* of those studies precisely because of its installed population; the i-FRAME was born to attack the no. 2 failure item (bearings) with embedded monitoring.
