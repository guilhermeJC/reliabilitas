Equipment that **extracts energy from the fluid** — pressure, velocity or enthalpy — and converts it into **shaft work**. They are the functional mirror of [[adicao-de-energia|Energy Addition to the Fluid]]: the same turbomachinery physics, traversed in the opposite direction.

## Families

- [[turbinas-hidraulicas|Hydraulic Turbines]] — extract energy from water heads and streams (Francis, Kaplan, Pelton, Bulb, Turgo).
- [[turbinas-a-vapor|Steam Turbines]] — expand boiler-generated steam (ISO 14224 class **ST**).
- [[turbinas-a-gas|Gas Turbines]] — Brayton cycle: expand combustion gases (class **GT**).
- [[expansores|Expanders]] — recover work from pressurized process gases (turboexpanders — class **TE** — and volumetric expanders).

Boundary note: steam and gas turbines also act as **drivers** of pumps and compressors. Classification here follows the **primary function on the machine's own working fluid** (extracting energy from it); the driver role is the service rendered to the driven equipment — see [[acionamento|Drivers]].
