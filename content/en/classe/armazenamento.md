Equipment whose function is to **hold inventory** — raw material, intermediates or product — without adding energy or transforming the fluid. It is the static frontier of the collection: the risk lies not in moving parts but in **loss of containment**.

Usual types *(own families and handbooks in Phase 1)*:

- **Atmospheric tanks** (API 650) — fixed and floating roof; they correspond to class **TA** (*storage tanks*) of ISO 14224:2016;
- **Pressure vessels** (ASME Section VIII) — accumulators, surge drums, process vessels;
- **LPG spheres and bullets** — liquefied gases under pressure.

From a reliability standpoint, **corrosion (internal and under insulation — CUI), nozzle fatigue and foundation/shell failure** dominate — slow mechanisms, with risk-based inspection (RBI) as the typical strategy.
