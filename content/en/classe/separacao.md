Equipment whose function is to **separate phases or components** of a process stream — solid from gas, solid from liquid, liquid from liquid or gas from liquid — by difference in density, size or permeability.

Usual types *(own families and handbooks in Phase 1)*:

- **Cyclones and hydrocyclones** — centrifugal separation with no moving parts;
- **Gravity separators** — settling vessels, oil-water separators (e.g. coalescing plates);
- **Filters** — filter press, bag filter, cartridge elements;
- **Membranes** — micro/ultra/nanofiltration and reverse osmosis;
- **Centrifuges and decanters** — rotation-accelerated separation.

In ISO 14224:2016, process separators appear in the **vessels and static equipment** branch; the functional group adopted here also gathers the rotating separation machines (centrifuges), following process-engineering practice — a declared adaptation, not a single class of the standard.
