Equipment whose function is to **raise the mechanical energy of the fluid** — as pressure, velocity or both. It is the largest functional group of fluid machinery in the process industries, and concentrates most of the maintenance cost of rotating assets.

## The working fluid defines the family

The fluid type is not a taxonomy level of its own — it is the **attribute that separates the families** in this group:

| Family | Working fluid | Typical pressure rise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bombas|Pumps]] | liquids — including multiphase mixtures and slurries | set by the system curve |
| [[compressores|Compressors]] | gases and vapors | pressure ratio > ~1.2 |
| [[sopradores|Blowers]] | gases | ratio ~1.1–1.2 |
| [[ventiladores|Fans]] | gases at high flow | small ΔP (ratio < ~1.1) |
| [[intensificadores-de-pressao|Pressure Intensifiers]] | liquids or gases | localized multiplication, no transport |

In ISO 14224:2016 (Annex A), the corresponding *equipment classes* include **PU** (pumps) and **CO** (compressors). Fans and blowers have no class of their own in the standard — they appear as subunits of other equipment (e.g. the fan of an *air cooler*) and are covered by AMCA/ASME PTC 11 practice; failure recording follows the same subdivision logic.
