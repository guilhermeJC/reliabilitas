Equipment whose function is to **transfer heat between streams** — heating, cooling, condensing or evaporating — usually across a separating surface, with no direct contact between the fluids. It corresponds to class **HE** (*heat exchangers*) of ISO 14224:2016.

Usual types in the process industries *(own families and handbooks in Phase 1)*:

- **Shell and tube** — the workhorse (TEMA construction standards);
- **Plate** (gasketed, brazed, welded) — compact, high effectiveness;
- **Spiral** — fouling and viscous fluids;
- **Air cooler** (API 661) — heat rejection without cooling water;
- **Condensers and evaporators** — purpose-built phase change;
- **Economizers, heaters and coolers** — heat recovery and thermal conditioning services.

From a reliability standpoint the group is dominated by **static** mechanisms: fouling, corrosion, tube erosion and gasket/tubesheet failure — unlike the rotating universe of [[adicao-de-energia|Energy Addition to the Fluid]].
