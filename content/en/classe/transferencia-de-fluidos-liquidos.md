Asset class that **adds energy to a liquid** to transfer it between process points — overcoming elevation, pressure differences and friction losses.

In the ISO 14224 taxonomy this is the rotating pumping equipment branch (*equipment class* PU). It is one of the largest installed populations and top maintenance cost drivers in industrial plants.
