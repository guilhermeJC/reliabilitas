Asset class that **adds energy to a liquid** — to transfer it between process points (overcoming elevation, pressure and friction) or to raise its pressure.

In the ISO 14224 taxonomy this is the rotating pumping equipment branch (*equipment class* PU). It is one of the largest installed populations and top maintenance cost drivers in industrial plants.

## Families

- [[bombas|Pumps]] — machines that move the liquid; the dominant family (>80% of rotating equipment).
- [[intensificadores-de-pressao|Pressure Intensifiers]] — multiply the pressure of a fluid (primary function: pressure, not transport).
