Equipment that **converts electrical or chemical energy into shaft work** to drive pumps, compressors, fans and other machines — it **never touches the process fluid**. Every rotating machine train is the *driver + driven* pair: the reliability of the set is that of the weakest link, and failure analysis must look at both sides of the coupling.

## Families

- [[motores-eletricos|Electric Motors]] — the dominant industrial driver (ISO 14224 class **EM**).
- **Combustion engines** (class **CE**) — diesel and gas, typical of emergency services and remote sites. *(family in Phase 1)*

Boundary note: **steam and gas turbines** also render driving service, but their primary function on their own working fluid is to extract energy from it — they live in [[remocao-de-energia|Energy Extraction from the Fluid]].
