Equipment whose function is to **homogenize** — combining streams, dispersing phases, keeping solids in suspension or promoting mass transfer by agitation.

Usual types *(own families and handbooks in Phase 1)*:

- **Tank agitators** — axial impellers (propeller, hydrofoil) and radial impellers (Rushton turbine, flat blades), selected by flow regime and viscosity;
- **Static mixers** — fixed in-line elements, mixing at the cost of head loss (no moving parts);
- **Dynamic in-line mixers** — rotor-stator for fine dispersions;
- **Homogenizers** — high pressure/high shear for stable emulsions.

ISO 14224:2016 **defines no dedicated class** for mixing equipment — this group is a declared adaptation, supported by process-engineering practice (Handbook of Industrial Mixing). From a reliability standpoint, agitated machines share the rotating fundamentals of [[adicao-de-energia|fluid machinery]]: sealing, bearings and unbalance dominate.
